Hatsune Miku: Virtual Pop Diva!

Hatsune Miku, codenamed CV01, is a virtual singer and one of the most recognizable digital characters in the world! SHe was released in 2007 by the Japanese company Crypton Future Media as a voicebank for the singing synthesis software Vocaloid! Her voice was created using recordings of Japanese voice actress Saki Fujita. Producers can use the software to type lyrics and melodies, and Miku will sing them! Because of this, thousands of musicians around the world have used her voice to create original songs in many different genres! Miku is often depicted as a 16 year old girl with long turquoise twin tails, futuristic clothing and a digital idol aesthetic!

Skills

Anybody can create songs using her voice! Therefore she can sing in many different styles like pop, rock, electronic and more. She can also sing in many different languages! Primarily Japanese but has also been adapted to sing in English and Chinese, making her music global! Since anyone can make songs with her voice, Miku has performed thousands of unique songs written by different producers worldwide!

Achievements

Miku's impact goes far beyond music software, she's become a worldwide culturall icon!! Songs featuring Miku have gained hundreds of millions of views online, showing her huge global fanbase! She has performed sold out shows across North America, Eurpoe and Asia! Albums featuring her voice have ranked on major music charts, including Japan's Oricon charts! She stars in popular SEGA rhythm game series, Hatsune Miku: Project DIVA and HATSUNE MIKU: COLORFUL STAGE! Miku has collaborated with global companies and even appeared in live performances with artists like Lady Gaga!
